About

Finance is complicated. The reasons behind it usually aren't the ones we talk about.

portrait
Reserve Bank of India BNP Paribas Nasdaq MBA (Finance), IIM Indore

I'm Pranit Saindani. Over the past decade, I've worked across financial regulation, banking, compliance and fintech, including roles at the Reserve Bank of India, BNP Paribas and Nasdaq.

Working across these institutions shaped how I think about finance and technology. I became interested in the gap between how systems are designed to work and what actually happens once incentives, regulation and human behaviour get involved.

This site is where I explore those ideas more broadly. I write about finance, regulation, technology, AI and human behaviour, particularly where they intersect and where the conventional explanation doesn't quite hold up.

I'm interested in understanding why systems behave the way they do, questioning assumptions that have become accepted as obvious, and making complicated ideas easier to think about without oversimplifying them.

These days, I'm also building Hilio, a financial wellbeing app exploring how behavioural and emotional patterns shape the way people manage money.
